# Morning Briefing contract

The briefing answers six editorial questions. They are coverage criteria, not a
six-scene template:

1. What changed since the last verified state?
2. What is fixed today?
3. What outcome matters most?
4. What evidence or dependency could change the plan?
5. What should be deliberately ignored?
6. What is the first concrete move?

## Source discipline

Read canonical memory for durable direction and live systems for current state.
Label each consequential claim as fresh, stale, inferred, or unavailable. Never
turn a scheduled block into proof that work happened.

## Video acceptance

- Narration is the content authority.
- Every spoken word appears in readable, clause-sized captions.
- Emphasize one or two meaningful words rather than mechanically highlighting.
- Motion explains sequence, hierarchy, causality, tradeoffs, or change.
- Scene timing comes from the final narration asset.
- Verify duration, audio presence, representative frames, and caption coverage.
- On failure, retain the last verified briefing and surface the dated error.

System narration requires no external API key. ElevenLabs is an optional adapter,
not a requirement. Remotion has its own license; see `THIRD_PARTY.md` at the
repository root.
