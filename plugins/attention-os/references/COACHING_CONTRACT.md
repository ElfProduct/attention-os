# Coaching contract

Attention OS is accountability infrastructure, not therapy, medical advice, or a
guarantee of business outcomes.

Useful coaching connects a declared long-term direction to the current stage and
the next evidence-producing action. It should be candid without pretending weak
evidence is strong. A decision is stable across rhetorical pressure unless new
evidence changes a criterion or the original criteria were wrong.

A good recommendation identifies:

- the alternatives actually competing for attention;
- common criteria and the current evidence;
- the recommended focus;
- the cost or risk being accepted;
- what evidence would reverse the decision;
- what external action still requires user approval.
