# Attention OS daily reconciliation

Do not give a daily verdict until the review window and evidence coverage are
explicit. Gather, when available and authorized: the stated plan and goal;
Calendar across the full local day; current tasks and external deadlines;
recorded activity across the full window; verified outputs, submissions, sales,
messages, or commits; and material off-device context from the user.

Record unavailable sources as gaps. Missing telemetry is not proof of inactivity.
Separate scheduled time, observed effort, draft output, external submission, and
verified resolution.

Deliver a concise verdict covering what materially advanced, what did not, why,
confidence, and one adjustment for tomorrow. Save a bounded review in `reviews/`
and update `reviews/latest.md` only after the evidence pass. Let the memory
compiler decide what becomes durable coaching state.
